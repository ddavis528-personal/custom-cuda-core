## Confirmed correct

Recorded so it is not re-asked.

**Checkpoints do not need migrating.** Demotion squashes to the retirement boundary, so no unresolved branch survives it and a warp's checkpoints are empty when its state moves. Worth stating explicitly, because A-2 just added `group_masks` to `ccv_fet_pca_mig` and the obvious next question is whether checkpoints belong there too. They do not.

**A-2's resolution still holds and is strengthened.** FET now definitively owns PC-group state, so FET migrating the group masks is the right block doing it rather than a convenient one.

**The fixed-latency contract is the right trade.** Removing the port grant and the late/cancel bit is only safe because latency is contractual, and the arrival-cycle checker per completion channel is what makes the contract testable rather than assumed. That checker is doing real work and should not be treated as redundant later.

**Deferred free is correct.** MIU completing every accepted memop even after a squash, with OOE holding the ROB slot and registers until completion, avoids the orphaned-response problem without a cancel protocol. The new outstanding-tag checker — no reallocation of a tag with a memop in flight — is the right way to hold it.

**Bulk discard reuses the A-10 pattern properly**: a discriminator with every other field reserved and zero, which is checkable, rather than fields that are "meaningless" in one mode.

**Generated latencies are the right mechanism.** Deriving execution latency as base plus 2N per repeated link from `params/links.json` ties the number to the flop-per-boundary rule, so a partitioning change cannot silently invalidate a latency the scheduler depends on.
